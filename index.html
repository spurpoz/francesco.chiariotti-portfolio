Francesco Chiariotti

Brand design and UX/UI.

Always fascinated by colors, shapes, and sounds, I mix my passions
with a desire to swim out into the open sea, often finding myself
holding my breath in the depths of a project.

I work across branding and UI, with a Swiss grid approach
and a tendency towards bold, vibrant colors.

I like dub music, vintage guitars, and alternative country roads. My hair is slowly turning grey.

Brand design and UX/UI.
From Italy. Connected worldwide.
