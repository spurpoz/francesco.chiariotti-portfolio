Francesco Chiariotti work info @2026

O/H — No Contact

artwork / print
2024

visit: bandcamp/no-contact

creative direction: Pausa Lab

Along with Marco, we designed the cover and prints for the two vinyl releases of the electronic-industrial punk project O/H, for the independent label KR3.

The project takes its name from a psychiatric hospital. Over the years, the duo has built a strong presence in the electronic-industrial scene.

No Contact stems from nine years of performances, spanning from 2011 to 2019.

We developed the concept of representing the hospital in a blurry, ambiguous way — somewhere between a lapse of memory and a grandfather’s postcard of an eerie place — shaping both the colour palette and the layout.

*All photographs of the duo were taken by other professionals.
