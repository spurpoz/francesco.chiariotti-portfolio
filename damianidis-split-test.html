Damianidis is an Athens-based furniture retailer specialising in contemporary designer sofas.

Working as part of the studio, we developed a new visual identity for the brand, including a refined logo system, the digital presence and the brand’s Instagram direction.

The experience was built entirely from scratch, with custom layouts, scroll-based animations, an image-led navigation system and a series of bespoke interactions designed to give the interface a distinct sense of movement and character.

The result is a digital identity that brings Damianidis’ physical presence into a contemporary online space — expressive, tactile, bold.
